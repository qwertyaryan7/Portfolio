File 01

The Giver of Siddhis

On the final day of Navratri, we worship Maa Siddhidatri, the bestower of supreme siddhis (perfections). She represents cosmic equality—a divine balance reflected in our republic's push for women-led governance.

From the 15 women in the Constituent Assembly to the historic 106th Amendment (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam), today we celebrate the milestones securing women's rightful place in leadership.
