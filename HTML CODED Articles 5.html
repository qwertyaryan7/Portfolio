Eligibility & Age Limits

Age limits are calculated as of October 28, 2026. Standard age relaxations apply: 5 years for SC candidates and 3 years for OBC candidates.

Senior Residents, Anaesthetists, Pathologists & CMOs

Qualification: Must hold a recognized Postgraduate Medical Degree (M.D./M.S./DNB/M.Ch./D.M) or equivalent.

Upper Age Limit: 45 years.

Demonstrators, MOs, EMOs, & LMOs

Qualification: Must hold an MBBS degree. Preference is given to PG degree holders. M.Sc. (Medical) candidates may be considered for certain Demonstrator specialties if medical candidates are unavailable.

Upper Age Limit: 37 years.

Junior Residents (MBBS & BDS)

Qualification: Must hold an MBBS/BDS degree, be registered with the State Medical Council or MCI, and complete a one-year rotatory internship by December 31, 2026.

Upper Age Limit: 30 years.

Application Fee & Process

Applications and document uploads must be submitted exclusively online through the GMCH website (www.gmch.gov.in) under the 'VACANCIES' section.

  • All Other Categories: ₹500 plus any applicable bank charges.
  • SC Candidates: ₹250 plus any applicable bank charges.
  • Exemptions: Persons with Disability (PwD) candidates and candidates sponsored by the Regional Employment Exchange (REE) are exempted.
Note: Candidates holding postgraduate qualifications who apply for multiple eligible departments only need to pay the requisite application fee once and will appear for a single related test.
